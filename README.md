# ISHT--Integrated-Smart-Himalayan-Threat-monitorig-system-

ISHT — Integrated Smart Himalayan Threat-monitoring is our proposed AI-based platform for early forest-fire detection and forest monitoring in Uttarakhand.

1. AI Valley Cameras — Early Detection

We will place high-resolution cameras at suitable elevated points overlooking valleys. The AI model will continuously analyse the camera feed and identify smoke or flames at an early stage, especially fires that may not be picked up by satellites.

Camera location reference:(   https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TXsNcaJfwmw6DwAEMSkLx9B36GtklBvw/view?usp=drivesdk   )

2. Satellite Monitoring — Wider Coverage

We will use SNPP-VIIRS (375 m) as the primary fire-hotspot data source and MODIS (1 km) as supporting data to monitor larger forest areas.

3. Drone Verification

If a camera or satellite detects a suspicious location, an authorized drone can be used to capture high-resolution or thermal images and verify the situation.

4. AI + GIS Dashboard

All camera, satellite and drone data will be brought together on a single GIS dashboard, showing the location, time, confidence level, affected area and verification status.

5. Alerts + Forest Health

Once a threat is verified, the system can alert the concerned forest teams. The same platform will also track vegetation and forest-cover changes to understand overall forest health.

Detect → Verify → Assess → Alert → Act
